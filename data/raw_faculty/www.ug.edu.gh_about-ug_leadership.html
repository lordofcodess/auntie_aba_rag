Prof Nana Aba Appiah Amfo Vice Chancellor

Prof Nana Aba Appiah Amfo

Vice Chancellor

Nana Aba Appiah Amfo, PhD, FGA, is the (first female) Vice-Chancellor of the University of Ghana. She is a Professor of Linguistics and an experienced university administrator, with over 21 years’ experience in the higher education sector.

Professor Amfo has built her experience in governance through attendance of formal management and leadership programs as well as on the job training. She brings a lot of innovation and resourcefulness to her leadership positions and is adept at change management. As Vice-Chancellor, she’s leading her university to uptake technology in all its operations, while keeping people as the focus. As part of the execution of her vision, she launched a seven-million-dollar digitalization program to enhance the experience of the University’s students, faculty and staff.

Professor Amfo previously served as the Pro Vice-Chancellor with responsibility for Academic and Student Affairs, the Dean of the School of Languages, and the Head of the Department of Linguistics, of the same university. Her masters and PhD degrees, both in linguistics, are from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) and her Bachelors in French and Linguistics is from the University of Ghana.

She is a member of many prestigious societies and organizations such as the Ghana Academy of Arts and Sciences, Commonwealth Professionals Fellowship, Alexander von Humboldt Foundation, German Academic Exchange Services, and the International Women’s Fellowship Leadership Program. She is a pioneer fellow of the African Humanities Program (AHP) of the American Council of Learned Societies (ACLS). She currently serves as the inaugural President of the African Humanities Association, an offshoot of the AHP.

Her national and international board membership include the following: Consultation Board Member of the International Pragmatics Association; Assistant Secretary-General of the Fédération Internationale des Langues et Littératures Modernes; Administrative Board Member of the International Association of Universities; Steering Committee Member of the Tallories Network of Engaged Universities, Chairperson of the Interim Executive Committee of the African Pragmatics Association and Governing Council Member of the Sustainable Development Solution Network.

In 2021, Professor Amfo was awarded as the GUBA (Grow Unite Build Africa) Nana Yaa Asantewaa Woman of Enlightenment for her resilience and work in academia which has created knowledge, built confidence and broken-down barriers to opportunities. In July 2023, She was recognised by Glitz Africa as the Ghana Woman of the Year for Excellence in Education.

Major Daniel Sowa Ablorh-Quarcoo (Rtd) Council Appointee

Major Daniel Sowa Ablorh-Quarcoo (Rtd)

Council Appointee

Major Daniel Sowa Ablorh-Quarcoo is a chartered management accountant. He served as Chairman of Ghana's Public Interest and Accountability Committee, as well as on the Council of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Ghana. In 2006, he was appointed as Commissioner of the Internal Revenue Service (IRS). During his tenure, he assisted five other African Commissioners in establishing the African Tax Administration Forum. Prior to joining the IRS, he served ten years (1994-2004) as Director of Finance for the Ghana Cocoa Board (COCOBOD), building on six previous years’ experience as a management accountant with COCOBOD. Prior to this time, he served eleven years as a Paymaster with the Ghana Armed Forces. 

He has a Bachelor of Science degree in Accounting from the University of Ghana Business School.

He chairs the University’s Finance and General Purposes Committee.

Council Appointee

Professor Folasade Tolulope Ogunsola Vice Chancellor of the University of Lagos

Professor Folasade Tolulope Ogunsola

Vice Chancellor of the University of Lagos

Professor Folasade Tolulope Ogunsola is an accomplished clinical microbiologist, and has been on the faculty of the University of Lagos since 2008. Her research interest has centered mainly around the diagnosis and prevention of infectious diseases as well as antibiotic resistance. She served as the first female Provost of the College of Medicine and the first female Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Development Services) at the University of Lagos. She is currently the 13th and first female Vice-Chancellor of the University of Lagos after 60 years of the University’s existence.  

She studied at the University of Ife (now Obafemi Awolowo University) for her Bachelor of Medicine and Surgery degree, obtained a Masters' degree in Medical Microbiology with distinction at the College of Medicine, University of Lagos, and then proceeded to the University of Wales, College of Medicine, where she obtained both a diploma in Biomedical Techniques and a PhD in Medical. 

From 2004 to 2022, she directed the PEPFAR-sponsored HIV reference laboratory, responsible for the diagnosis and monitoring of over 20,000 HIV-infected patients. Professor Ogunsola is a mentor and advocate for women taking up leadership positions and has over 145 peer-reviewed publications in national and international journals. 

Rev. Fr. Stephen Owusu-Sekyere CHASS Representative

Rev. Fr. Stephen Owusu-Sekyere

CHASS Representative

Rev. Fr. Stephen Owusu Sekyere obtained a Bachelor of Arts degree in 2002 at the University of Ghana; and a Post Graduate Diploma in Education in 2004 at the University of Cape Coast. In 2012, he was awarded a Master of Science degree in Educational Leadership by Madonna University, Livonia, Michigan, USA, and trained as a high school principal. 

On 13th July 2003, he was ordained a Deacon and ordained as a Priest of the Catholic Church on 17th July 2004 in the Kumasi Archdiocese. 

He has held various leadership positions, including Domestic Bursar, helping Priest, resident Priest, Rector/Headmaster, and Chairman of the Kumasi Archdiocesan Pastoral Team from 2021-present. 

Rev. Fr. Stephen Owusu Sekyere is currently the Headmaster of Opoku Ware Senior High School and, since 2019, President of the Conference of Heads of Assisted Secondary Schools (CHASS). He is a member of the West African Examinations Council (WAEC), and Member of the Ghana Administrative and Finance Committee of WAEC.
