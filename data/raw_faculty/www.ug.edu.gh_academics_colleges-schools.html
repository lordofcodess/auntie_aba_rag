Colleges & Schools

College of Basic and Applied Sciences

This college aims at developing world-class scientists to meet national and global developmental needs through quality teaching, learning, research, innovation, and extension.

Explore the college

College of Education

This college exposes students to best practices in teaching and learning using the latest proven learning technologies and supports the utilization of research both to inform teaching and to tackle the broader issues of education.

Explore the college

College of Health Sciences

The College of Health Sciences aims to promote health through education, research, and service in order to produce highly qualified and competent health professionals and medical scientists.
