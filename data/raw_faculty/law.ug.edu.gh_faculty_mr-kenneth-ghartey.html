About

Kenneth Ghartey is Lecturer in the University of Ghana School of Law. Kenneth is a lawyer and a Valuation and Estate Surveyor by training and is licensed as an Insolvency Practitioner in Ghana by the Office of the Registrar of Companies, Ghana. He started his teaching career as a Lecturer in Law at Lancaster University Ghana where he later became Senior Lecturer and Head of the Law Department. He has significant corporate and commercial legal advisory and consulting experience. He has been involved in major transactions including a sovereign bond issue and a multi-million-dollar petroleum-related international arbitration. Kenneth was the sole Technical Consultant for the preparation of the text for the User Guides for recent Ghanaian company and insolvency legislation under the World Bank Group (IFC) Ghana Investment Climate Project. Kenneth is a recognised expert on the company law of Ghana and his work on directors’ duties has been read over 15,000 times on research websites. Kenneth has a keen interest and experience in providing training for professionals in the areas of his expertise.

Kenneth was an Editor of the maiden volume of the Corporate Insolvency and Restructuring Journal hosted by the Ghana Association of Restructuring and Insolvency Advisors [GARIA] in 2023. He has also served on the Editorial Committee of the INSOL International I-Read Student Newsletter since 2023.
