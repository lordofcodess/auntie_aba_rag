About

Jennifer Hall is a lecturer with the University of Ghana School of Law who previously taught Company & Business Law at the University of Professional Studies. She is particularly interested in the academic discourse that seeks to assess how International Law and Human Rights Law can be applied to advance the interests of African countries, especially Ghana.

Her current research focuses on Third World Approaches to International Law, Poverty and Human Rights Law, Business and Human Rights Law and Criminal Law.

Credentials

  • LLM- London School of Economics and Political Science, London, UK
  • QCL- The Ghana School of Law, Accra
  • LLB- School of Law, University of Ghana, Legon, Ghana

Areas of Expertise

  • Criminal Law
  • Law of Contract
  • International Human Rights Law
