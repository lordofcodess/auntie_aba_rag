PROFILE 

Abdul-Jalilu Ateku's major research interests are in international relations theories, international security interventions in war-affected/post-war states – peacebuilding, peacekeeping, human security, human rights, elections, election security and democracy, and African politics. More recently, his attention has focused on international security interventions in war/post-war-affected states, particularly the impact of peacebuilding on human security in war-affected states in Africa.
