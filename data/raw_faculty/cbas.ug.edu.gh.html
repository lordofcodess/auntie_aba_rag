    Professor Sandow Mark Yidana

    Provost · College of Basic and Applied Sciences

    Welcome to the College of Basic and Applied Sciences, University of Ghana.

    It is with great pleasure that I welcome you to our vibrant academic community. At the College of Basic and Applied Sciences, we are dedicated to fostering excellence in education, groundbreaking research, and meaningful service.

    Our faculty members are leaders in their fields, committed to providing our students with the knowledge, skills, and experiences necessary to succeed in today's rapidly evolving world. Through innovative teaching methods and cutting-edge research opportunities, we prepare our students to tackle the grand challenges facing society.

    Whether you are a prospective student, current student, faculty member, alumnus, or community partner, I invite you to explore our website and learn more about the exciting initiatives and opportunities available within our college.
