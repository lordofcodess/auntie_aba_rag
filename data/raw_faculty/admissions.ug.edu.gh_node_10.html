Overview

Welcome to the Graduate Admissions of the University of Ghana. The School of Graduate Studies offers a wide range of post-graduate programmes in Agriculture & Consumer Science, Arts, Business, Engineering Science, Health Sciences, Law, Science and Social Sciences. 

It is announced for the information of prospective applicants and the general public that the School of Graduate Studies is offering various Masters/PhD programmes for the 2023-2024 Academic Year.
