WES Evaluation

All requests for WES evaluation of academic records must now be made through the STS portal: https://sts.ug.edu.gh.

The fee for electronically sending your transcript/academic records to WES is 10 US dollars per academic program, payable via the STS portal.

Once payment is made, please upload the following documents on the portal to complete your request:

  • A scanned copy of your degree certificate(s)
  • A signed copy of your filled Academic Records Request Form from WES

You may download the WES Academic Records Request Form from:
https://applications.wes.org/onlineapp/pdf/International_Transcript_Request.pdf

We will begin processing your request as soon as all required documents are submitted via the portal.

For assistance or enquiries, kindly contact us at academic@ug.edu.gh.

Please note: WES requests are processed within two weeks.
